# Google Play screenshot plan

## Required and recommended set

Google Play requires at least two screenshots across device types. For strong phone-store eligibility, prepare **eight portrait phone screenshots at 1080 × 1920px**. Play permits up to eight per device type.

The files must be JPEG or 24-bit PNG without alpha. The long edge cannot exceed twice the short edge. They must show the actual app experience.

## Capture rules

- Capture the exact release AAB on a clean emulator/device.
- Use dark appearance consistently unless showing theme choice is itself the point.
- Remove carrier name, personal notifications and real member information.
- Show full battery, Wi-Fi and cellular indicators where present.
- Do not show debug banners, developer menus, localhost URLs or staging labels.
- Keep the first three images strongly focused on real app UI.
- Avoid device frames and Play badges.
- Overlay text must be localised, exact and outside important UI.
- Export with no alpha and verify dimensions mechanically.

## Tablet decision

The Android app can run on larger screens, but tablet listing assets should be added only after the layouts pass large-screen QA. Google recommends at least four 9:16 or 16:9 screenshots between 1080 and 7680px for tablet/Chromebook promotion. Do not upscale phone captures and present them as tablet UI.
